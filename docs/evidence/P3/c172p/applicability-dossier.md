The normal-reference matrix names all missing conditions rather than supplying default ISA/calm/level/paved/full-rich assumptions. Cruise separates TAS, RPM and power; fuel mass/time requires density and explicit source-unit conversion; climb pairs IAS with rate; stall requires speed definition, weight/CG, flaps, power and bank/load factor. Ground roll and specified-obstacle distance are distinct for both takeoff and landing; obstacle height stays null until verified. Note4's historical CAS marking convention does not make arbitrary IAS equal CAS. Numerical trim convergence is a solver property, not independent aircraft trim truth.
