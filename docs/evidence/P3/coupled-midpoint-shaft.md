## Purpose and implementation

The [previous lifecycle trial](event-aware-shaft.md) failed three frozen shaft-convergence checks during starter cranking and coast-down. This corrective implementation models the actual starter torque, speed-dependent engine losses and propeller load with `event_aware_coupled_midpoint_v1`. The original twelve lifecycle cases, schedules and physical limits remain unchanged. The numerical correction and player adoption have separate acceptance gates.

The method selects combustion eligibility before constructing the held engine law. One public engine call retains the original manifold, airflow, fuel and thermal chronology. A bounded implicit-midpoint solve handles aerodynamic and starter boundaries and finite stops; its scalar helpers cannot call engine or fuel code. The historical default and held-power methods remain separate. The current playable package has not adopted this implementation.
