# ADR 011: Bounded observed flight review

Date: 2026-10-04. Status: proposed; consumers wait for a reviewed contract PR and coordinator dispatch. Contract issue [#132](https://github.com/brettbergin/flight-simulator/issues/132). This is a partial prerequisite for PRD-025, UX-017 and UX-021 and the engineering experience behind #27 and #75. It closes neither issue nor a phase gate. Basis: accepted [ADR006](006-interactive-prototype.md), [ADR007](007-sim-loop-facade.md), [ADR008](008-input-presets.md), [ADR009](009-cockpit-readings.md), and [v1 contracts](../contracts.md), at accepted main `2fac942bbc5a7574926949daca42bae9c1465d27`.

## Outcome and limits

After flying, the player can pause and review a sampled path, speed/height/fuel timeline and the held pilot controls at an observed instant. Review is local presentation of copied observations. It neither runs a second executive nor restores the current airplane to an earlier state.

Use one in-memory recording for the accepted original interactive model, fixed prepared plane, seed 42 and runtime 120 Hz. Target two observations per simulated second, retain at most 2,401 samples over at most 144,000 ticks (20 simulated minutes), and report skipped observation targets explicitly. Simulation pause contributes no additional flight time or sample. Exit discards the recording; no file, database, automatic persistence, import/export or profile is introduced.

Discard confirmations guard deliberate actions inside the running application only. An unexpected kill, crash, forced shutdown or other process termination loses this memory; there is no durable record or recovery promise.

This leaf records sampled truth and held axes, not complete pilot command arrivals, applied command history, operational event history, procedural flight phases, touchdown instants or a deterministic replay recipe. A sampled brake value is an observed held control, not proof of the pilot's intention or a checklist action. Labels say **RECORDED OBSERVATIONS / ORIGINAL PROTOTYPE**. There is no scoring, completion award, aircraft-limit assessment, training credit or C172 fidelity claim.

The [flight-only reconstruction proof](../evidence/P1/save-proof.md) does not establish combined ground-flight resume. Do not emit SessionManifest, ReplayHeader, Checkpoint or TrainingResult declarations for this app value. Their existing public wire layouts and registry fingerprints remain unchanged.

## Authority and input qualification

The recorder and review leaf belong to their construction/main thread. Foreign-thread access rejects before reading or mutating owner data. They have no facade, worker, mapper, native session, camera, aircraft Node3D, origin registry or filesystem reference. The scene supplies owned ADR007 Readbacks after its normal result adoption. It remains the only authority for pause, controls, reset, close and scene origins.

Qualify every observation through `NativeReadings.from_readback` and the complete ADR009 input checks, not selected numeric fields alone. Require a live or ordinarily paused current publication, `historical=false`, matching valid aircraft/weather/session/tick, runtime 120 Hz, the exact ADR006 model/prepared-world identity and `readback.atmosphere.seed == "42"`. AircraftSnapshot/v1 has no seed field; do not invent `aircraft.seed`. Sample time comes from `readback.aircraft.elapsed_s`; ADR007 has no top-level Readback elapsed_s. Require a valid nonnull Canonical value whose ECEF position agrees with the copied aircraft; reuse the accepted canonical-frame checks. Store its binary64 `anchor_eus_position_m`, never VisualPose, interpolated position, render-origin coordinates or float32 Vector3 values.

Bind the recording's identity from its first qualified publication. Its metadata contains only actually supplied identities: copied session ID, model ID/version/backend name, source fingerprint, prepared-world SHA256, world anchor, seed, clock and named start. No caller-invented executable/library hash, validated aircraft label, scenario ID or complete archive provenance is substituted for missing information. Later observations must preserve these bound identities exactly. Render-origin adoption does not change this fixed anchor.

A missing fuel channel or unavailable Euler heading/bank is preserved as ADR009's invalid channel with null value and its reason; it does not become zero or invalidate otherwise qualified channels. A malformed whole ReadingSet, unqualified Readback or nonfinite geometry cannot enter a sample. Samples and returned values recursively own their dictionaries/arrays. No returned reference can change retained data or live state.

## Recording lifetime

The ordinary scene begins one recorder after a successful new-session initialization and canonical adoption, normally at tick `"0"`. `begin` also accepts a qualified later first publication so a delayed attachment is honest about its actual start tick; it never reconstructs an omitted prefix. That recording's 20-minute window starts at this actual first tick. A first_tick other than `"0"` requires the visible **Recording starts at native tick ...; earlier flight not recorded** label; sample time is not relabeled as session initialization.

States are `empty`, `recording` and `sealed`. Empty contains null metadata and no samples. Recording starts with one actual sample at its first tick. Sealed preserves the valid prefix for review and accepts no further samples. Seal reasons are `manual`, `limit`, `terminal`, `closed`, `invalid_observation`, `identity_changed` and `tick_exhausted`; no reason means successful flight completion. Repeated seal of a sealed record is an unchanged no-op.

Ordinary pause/resume, scale changes, Controls, instrument focus and the route board retain the recording. Review opening/dismissal does not seal it. Repeated observations of an unchanged native tick add no sample, even when pause/scale/debt metadata changes. At one session/tick, physics, weather, held axes, canonical position and derived channel values must remain the same; contradictory values seal with `invalid_observation` before replacement. The recorder keeps one bounded last-qualified observation for this check, separate from the retained sample list.

`last_observed_tick` is the latest monotonically ordered tick from a qualified same-session publication, including valid retained terminal/closed truth. It may be later than the last retained sample. Every qualified current observation updates it even when no sample is due; sealed records never update it again. Counts and recording summaries distinguish this endpoint from `samples.back().tick`.

Known terminal/historical or confirmed closed Readbacks seal the valid prefix with `terminal` or `closed`; they add no sample. First qualify the complete historical Readback through ADR009: require its valid retained pair, matching bound identities and nonregressing tick, but allow the canonical=null case explicitly permitted after close/terminal handling. A qualified newer historical endpoint updates last_observed_tick and counts due targets not captured before that endpoint. It does not create missing positional/channel observations. A valid empty closed Readback supplies no new tick; while recording, it seals with closed at the prior qualified endpoint; while empty, it is a no-op.

A foreign session or changed identity never silently clears or replaces a recording: seal with `identity_changed`, preserve its prefix and reject the observation. Its tick does not become the old recording's endpoint. A malformed input, contradictory same-tick data or backward tick order seals with `invalid_observation`; preserve the previous last_observed_tick and qualified samples, count tails only through that previously verified endpoint, and report an error. Do not use an unvalidated input's tick/time to imply that additional flight was observed. The current UI must not present retained samples as live truth.

An owner-thread rejection while empty preserves the canonical empty Recording/Status, including their empty error; the diagnostic belongs to MutationResult.error. With a nonempty prefix, the specified rejection seal may retain its bounded diagnostic in Recording.error and Status.error.

There is only one recording, not an unbounded archive or a second previous-session buffer. Once last_observed_tick has advanced beyond first_tick, restart, another named start or quit must show that review will be discarded and require the player's explicit **Discard and restart** or **Discard and quit** action. The confirmation opens only after the scene's existing normal pause has established its verified boundary, or after confirmed joined close. Cancel is the default and leaves that paused/closed native state and recording exactly unchanged; it never clears data or silently resumes. For a request from live flight, the ordinary explicit pause happens first and its normal observation hook is the only pre-confirmation change. An initialization-only recording needs no additional discard confirmation.

The scene binds confirmation to the current recorded session and requested next action. `replace_confirmed` is a scene-owned authorization flag for this paused lifecycle choice, not a claim that a leaf can establish physics pause or authorize an arbitrary reset. The recorder has no native capability and does not acquire or verify a pause by calling the facade. No auto-confirm, default destructive action or hidden reset is permitted.

For restart, keep the prior sealed/paused recording until the existing close/join and new initialization succeed. Only a qualified new publication plus the explicit replacement decision can replace it. Failed close/join or failed new initialization preserves the old review and the existing failure behavior. Canceling a chooser changes nothing. On confirmed quit, retire it with the existing joined shutdown. No surprise automatic replacement is allowed if an unrelated caller creates a fresh session.

## Sampling and gaps

Ticks remain canonical decimal uint64 strings. Consume `U64.valid` and `U64.compare` for existing validation/order semantics; never convert an absolute tick or identity to signed int or binary64. The existing `U64.small_difference` is capped at 4096 and increments repeatedly; it is not the 144,000-tick recording arithmetic implementation. Add private leaf helpers with at most 20 decimal digit iterations: checked right-to-left subtraction with borrow, and checked addition of a small nonnegative integer with carry. Reject malformed input, negative/reversed differences and addition overflow. Validate the resulting canonical decimal string against U64 rules, compare a difference to `"144000"` before converting that bounded result to int, and reject out-of-bound conversion. Preserve the shared uint64 module and its existing contract unchanged. Bucket indices and late/skipped counts are bounded small integers.

Use checked add-small for T0+60 and grid offsets up to 144000. If a future target would exceed uint64, retain the prefix and seal with `tick_exhausted`; do not wrap or manufacture a final tick. Near maximum, count only representable intended grid targets that are due through the last verified tick. A large absolute tick and a large out-of-window difference must still be handled with bounded decimal work, not a per-tick loop.

Let the first actual tick be T0. Intended targets are T0, T0+60, T0+120, through T0+144000. The first sample is exact at T0. On a later qualified observation at t:

1. Validate identity, same-tick consistency and monotonic order before changing sampling state.
2. If t precedes the next target, retain only the bounded last-qualified observation; append nothing.
3. If t is within the recording window and at/after a target, append **at most one actual sample**. Its intended target is the latest grid tick at/before t; `late_by_ticks` is the checked difference from that target (0..59). Count intervening intended targets that were not captured as `skipped_targets_before`.
4. Advance the next target to the subsequent grid tick. Never duplicate the current snapshot for earlier missed targets, fabricate an exact-grid state or interpolate a recorded value.
5. At an exact observed final target, retain that actual sample then seal with `limit`. An observation beyond the window updates last_observed_tick from its qualified identity/tick, then seals without appending its out-of-window state. Its counting endpoint is capped at T0+144000, so unobserved targets beyond this recording window are not counted. Manual/terminal/closed seals count only through last_observed_tick, also clipped to the window; malformed/foreign input uses the prior qualified endpoint as specified above.

`uncaptured_tail_targets` is the number of due grid targets after the last retained sample's target through the counting endpoint, inclusive of an uncaptured final target. During recording it is zero: a qualified current observation at/after a due target captures the latest due target. On seal, compute the tail once without fabricating samples. `skipped_target_count` equals the sum of all Sample.skipped_targets_before plus uncaptured_tail_targets; do not count the same missed target twice. The first target is already represented by the first sample. Last-observed time can extend beyond the last sampled time; show this unsampled tail distinctly.

Each sample names its actual tick and intended target. `gap_before` is true exactly when `skipped_targets_before>0`; lateness alone does not mean a missing target. First sample has all three fields zero/false. Recording-level `skipped_target_count` sums uncaptured targets, while `late_sample_count` counts late observations separately. For example, T0=0 followed by t=61 captures target60 with late_by_ticks1 and no gap; a following t=181 captures target180, misses target120, and has skipped_targets_before1/gap_beforetrue. Display **2 Hz target / observed states**, with distinct late and gap markers. Distinct frame cadences may yield distinct observed sample ticks; do not claim exact sampling at grid ticks or frame-independent traces when the scene supplies only frame-observed publications.

The 2,401-sample cap follows from the unique grid targets and first sample. Reaching a bound visibly seals the recorder and reports its reason; it must not pause flight, overwrite the oldest sample, silently drop new observations or allocate another recording. A later reset remains an explicit lifecycle action.

## Owned app values and callable seam

These are closed in-process app values with contract version 1, not an importable file format. Unknown keys, invalid enums/types/ranges and mutable Objects/Resources reject. Normal application dictionaries are binary64 numeric values; tick identities are Strings.

`ObservedFlightRecorder.begin(readback:Variant, replace_confirmed:bool=false)->MutationResult` validates before binding/capturing. Nonempty replacement requires true and a different qualified session; same-session replacement rejects. The scene owns the explicit confirmation described above. `observe(readback:Variant)->MutationResult` performs qualification/sampling/sealing. An empty recorder rejects qualified live observations until begin; empty/closed observation is an unchanged no-op. A sealed recorder rejects further current observations without modifying its prefix.

`seal(reason:String)->MutationResult` accepts only `manual` or `closed` from the scene; other reasons are derived by qualification/bounds. `status()->RecorderStatus|null` returns a bounded owned copy on the owner thread, including a canonical empty status when empty. `recording()->Recording|null` returns the full deep owned copy on the owner thread, including a canonical empty Recording when empty, only on an explicit paused/stopped review request under the scene gate below; the scene does not poll it each frame. These methods return null only for foreign-thread access, before reading owner state.

Every owner-thread observe/begin/seal result returns only its typed status, never the retained sample list. A foreign-thread mutation returns exactly `{ok:false,error:"Observed flight review belongs to its main-thread owner",appended:false,status:null}` without reading or mutating owner state. No per-frame full-history copying or serialization is allowed. There is no method to set metadata, inject samples, seek a solver or append unvalidated caller-made readings. There is no public arbitrary clear operation: explicit different-session replacement or confirmed exit owns discard.

`ObservedReview.set_recording(recording:Variant)->bool` validates/copies the full closed Recording and clears selection on rejection; malformed input never leaves earlier values looking current. `select(index:int)->ReviewSelection` selects an existing sample by bounded index and returns owned historical data; unknown index returns unavailable without changing a valid selection. `selection()->ReviewSelection` returns the owned selected value. Replacing a different recording resets selection; initial valid selection defaults to the latest sample. These operations have no external side effects.

Foreign-thread review calls reject before reading or mutating owner state: set_recording returns false without clearing owner data, while select/selection return exactly `{available:false,error:"Observed flight review belongs to its main-thread owner",sample_index:null,sample:null,historical:true}`. This rejection is not an empty or invalid owner recording and cannot inspect or change its selection.

## Review UI and measurements

Open, scrub and dismiss review only while the existing scene is verified ordinarily paused, or after its existing close has confirmed a joined worker. The recorder/review never obtains pause itself. Opening from live flight uses the normal explicit scene pause before UI interaction; capture any resulting qualified boundary through the ordinary observation hook. Terminal states must pass the existing close/join policy before opening a stopped review. Selecting a sample, moving a cursor or dismissing cannot call facade/mapper/native mutators, move the real camera/ownship, change input edges/latches, alter held axes/debt, or adopt a different origin. Resume is the existing deliberate scene action.

Draw a separate north-up **recorded** map and graphs from stored fixed-anchor scalar coordinates and sampled readings. Do not replace the live locator's ownship or route state. Chart unavailable channels as gaps and suppress invalid numeric labels; do not join lines across `gap_before` or an uncaptured tail. Scrubbing displays an actual captured sample, not an interpolated physical state. The time cursor is based on checked bounded relative ticks divided by 120; paused wall time is not counted.

Permitted summaries distinguish first tick, last sampled tick and last_observed_tick; they also show sample/late/skipped counts, sampled channel minima/maxima, signed first-to-last sampled fuel change when both endpoint channels are valid, and **sampled planar track length**. Relative sampled times and the counting-endpoint span use only proven differences up to 144000 divided by120. If last_observed_tick lies beyond that window, display its exact tick string plus an explicit window-clipped endpoint; do not convert its arbitrary difference to int/float or imply an out-of-window recorded time. The track measure sums binary64 horizontal straight chords between successive valid captured samples with no skipped-target gap, including late actual samples. Each chord connects their real recorded instants and does not assume an exact 60-tick separation. Break at hard gaps; do not bridge or measure an uncaptured tail. This is sampled track length, not actual distance flown or a trajectory bound. No automatic leg, takeoff, touchdown, go-around, crash, safety outcome or achieved objective is inferred between samples. Missing data is explicit rather than zero-filled. Any derived nonfinite result is unavailable with a reason.

## Consumer ownership and evidence gate

Proposed leaf ownership is `app/replay/observed/`, `app/ui/debrief/observed/` and `tests/debrief/observed/`. Root coordinates `app/simulation/flight_scene.gd`, pause/reset/quit confirmation and map integration. Delivery owns exact package/staging source bindings. The current route feature, original model/provider/bridge, public schemas and input preset versions stay independently owned. Consumers wait for this contract and their own bounded issue; #127 piston runtime is not a dependency.

Before observing consumer output, freeze independent target/gap/full-range-string/path/units expectations. Private arithmetic fixtures cover malformed decimal strings, negative/reversed differences, zero/exact144000/exceeds-bound subtraction, full maximum uint64, borrow/carry across digit lengths, and near-maximum addition overflow without per-tick work. Verify bounded mutation/status responses contain no sample history, and full copies occur only on explicit review. Verify later-start prefix labeling, qualified historical endpoints versus malformed ticks, terminal/empty inputs, same-tick conflicts, identity changes, copies, pause/scale/rebase invariance, exact/late/skipped/limit/tail counts and failed-reset/Cancel preservation. Actual-native fixtures must prove record/review operations preserve readback, held controls, fractional debt, mapper state, command/lifecycle counts, origin and joined ownership. Test UI bounds and actual exported recorded/gap/unavailable/limit/reset-confirmation views at 960x540 and larger supported windows with exact staged source/package provenance. No native simulation rebuild is implied by this app-only contract; protected checks and affected consumer/package checks still apply.

Durable profiles/logs, export/import, atomic writes/recovery, full event history, physics reconstruction, combined-state saves, sensors, C172 procedures, scoring and pilot/phase acceptance remain separate requirements. A successful sample review cannot close their parent issues.
