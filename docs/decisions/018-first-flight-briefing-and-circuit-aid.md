# ADR 018: Bounded first-flight briefing and synthetic circuit reference

Status: **proposed**. Proposed by [#165](https://github.com/brettbergin/flight-simulator/issues/165); consumers are [briefing #166](https://github.com/brettbergin/flight-simulator/issues/166) and [circuit reference #167](https://github.com/brettbergin/flight-simulator/issues/167). Consumers wait for this checked contract PR, accepted issue158 delivery and coordinator dispatch. This is a bounded prototype contribution to #26/#27 and a small part of #22; it accepts no broad issue, owner/phase gate, aircraft, hardware, pilot or training credit.

Basis: accepted ADR006/007/008/009/010/011/012/013/017. Preserve native/model/120Hz/wire/preset/archive bytes and their existing authority. No general scenario framework, evaluator, persistence schema, new InputPreset action, physics parameter or automatic pilot is introduced.

## Player result and exact supported choices

Add a **First flight** entry while retaining ordinary free flight. It opens only through the existing verified pause/menu owner. Offer these three case-sensitive local choice IDs; these are menu selections, not new native named starts:

| Local choice | Exact profile ID | Exact named start | Wind | Honest description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cold-familiarization` | `original-piston-prop-v1` | `piston-cold-ground` | `calm` | Original cold piston; engine controls are available; idealized starter supply; no aircraft-specific checklist; recorded review unavailable. |
| `ready-flight` | `original-interactive-prototype` | `ground-ready` | `calm` | Original ready-to-fly model, engine already running; startup procedures skipped; legacy observed review available. |
| `airborne-orientation` | `original-interactive-prototype` | `airborne-prepared` | `calm` | Existing explicitly prepared airborne model; start/taxi/takeoff skipped; actual initialized state may differ from the requested trim recipe. |

Profiles mean the complete existing Facade profile dictionaries, not an ID alone with fabricated version/backend. Show actual adopted Readback identity/start/weather after success. A selection is a draft until ordinary initialization and scene adoption succeed. Do not call the cold profile ready-to-fly, add a cold airborne/running start, auto-start its engine, reposition the airplane or silently substitute a different profile/wind.

## Two small consumers and ownership

Consumer A is a paused briefing/orientation panel. It shows the supported choices, current remapped controls, actual start state and software familiarization steps. Consumer B is an optional fixed original circuit diagram on the existing locator plus a small reference card. Each is a copied presentation and signal producer; neither owns a Facade, mapper, native worker or device polling. Root alone integrates scene/map/lifecycle and staging/compiler/package hooks.

After the checked ADR018 contract and issue158 delivery are accepted, coordinator-dispatched agents may implement A and B in parallel within their disjoint leaf paths. Root joins them in **one cohesive dependency-group PR** and serializes shared files. B does not require a separately merged A PR before leaf implementation; actual A integration and its lifecycle/briefing tests are mandatory before the grouped PR can merge. Confirm every other named dependency and both leaves' acceptance/evidence independently; grouping bypasses no prerequisite, protected check or broad gate.

Use original frozen fixture files, not a new public content schema or generic loader: `content/scenarios/first-flight/briefing.json` and `content/world/synthetic/practice-circuit.json`. Their exact bytes/hash, provenance and literal bounded shape are frozen in tests before runtime observation. Unknown choice/revision, changed fixture or incompatible world fails visibly, rather than being normalized or treated as another exercise. Source/byte drift is a content change, not an opportunity to fit expected results.

### Briefing panel seam

`FirstFlightPanel.set_state(readback:Dictionary, preset:Dictionary, current_wind:String)->void` accepts recursively owned copies. Reuse ADR009 full Readback qualification and the correct existing `Preset.validate_preset` / `validate_preset_v2` for its actual version. Invalid/current/historical distinctions remain visible; unavailable bindings never become defaults. The scene supplies only its adopted `current_wind_profile`, not `wind_draft` as current truth. The panel may keep one bounded software step index; its meaning is manual familiarization, never an observed aircraft or checklist phase.

Signals: `choice_requested(choice_id:String, source_session:String)`, `controls_requested(source_session:String)`, `back_requested(source_session:String)`. Source session is the copied current session, or empty when no qualified session exists. Scene handlers bind the emitting widget and require current widget/session plus its existing verified pause/join, no-file-operation, no-initialization and no-pending-decision boundary. Repeated/stale/unknown requests cannot replace a newer flight. The panel cannot request native values, arbitrary starts or Resume.

Binding help is a pure display helper over the copied validated preset. Show physical-key names, mouse button IDs, axis fixed/key-pair/joy assignment and reversal. Label joystick slot/index bindings **CONFIGURED**. Current device availability and observation are **unverified here; open paused Controls to inspect observed status**. Missing/invalid bindings remain unavailable; a stored selector proves neither connection nor disconnection. Do not poll devices, infer availability, add diagnostics inputs to this seam or fabricate defaults. Reuse `pointer_source_hint` and Controls binding conventions. Relevant existing actions: `pause_menu`, `controls_panel`, `look_hold`, `view_cycle`, `view_cockpit`, `view_panel`, `recenter`, `map_toggle`, `runway_toggle`, `map_zoom_in`, `map_zoom_out`, `brake_hold`, `both_brakes`, `idle`; relevant axes: roll/pitch/yaw/throttle/mixture/trim. All hints follow validated remappings. Piston system hints come only from actual v2 systems. Escape retains its reserved pause/menu role.

Opening from live flight performs only the ordinary explicit pause before another Run. The panel uses `menu_open` ownership and existing pointer invalidation, never Raw suppression or manufactured button-up. Already-paused step, choice-draft, Back and tooltip changes introduce no command or lifecycle call. Controls opens through the existing paused method. Back returns to the menu still paused; only explicit ordinary Resume can resume.

A confirmed new selection reuses validation-before-close, joined replacement, existing recorder discard/default-Cancel and failure semantics. On successful adoption, stay paused at tick0 and show the actual briefing; the user explicitly chooses Resume. **Integration seam:** current `perform_start()` can call `close_menu()` for the same profile, so the first-flight path must carry a bounded scene-owned stay-paused continuation through immediate and confirmed-discard paths. Do not resume and then pause, create hidden ticks, or change other start paths. Cancel preserves the current paused session and recording; failed adopted replacement reports joined/closed truth and does not pretend the old session remains live.

### Circuit reference seam

First scope is **ready-flight / ground-ready / calm / runway36**, exact accepted synthetic world SHA `04bff5a0bcf3509990f6276b2548a28268f57fc96d218a7ca51cab1990ec1ff5`. This deliberately reuses the existing qualified legacy wind/readings/map domain. Cold-piston and airborne starts remain available in Consumer A but show **Circuit reference unavailable for this start**. Do not extend the legacy-only WindCue domain or cold review as part of this leaf.

`CircuitGeometry.view(readback:Variant, current_wind:String, selected_runway:int)->Dictionary` is pure. Reuse ADR009 full qualification and the accepted ADR013 `WindCue.from_readback` source/atmosphere checks. Require current live/paused truth, exact legacy profile, named start `ground-ready`, matching world/source, adopted wind `calm`, actual admitted wind/turbulence zero and selected runway36. No arbitrary epsilon reclassifies a nonzero wind as calm. Unknown/malformed/historical/retained/blocked/closed or incompatible input yields unavailable guidance with no stale ownship.

Return owned exact local display keys: `available:bool`, `state:String` (live/paused/unavailable), `session_id:String|null`, `tick:String|null`, `fixture_id:String`, `fixture_revision:int` (1), `ownship_anchor_eus_m:Array|null`, `points_anchor_eus_m:Array`, `leg_labels:Array`, `error:String`. Unavailable output has null ownship/session/tick and empty point/label arrays, with a nonempty bounded reason. Valid output copies all three canonical anchor-EUS scalars directly; absolute tick remains a decimal String. Fixture points are six fixed map-plane triples; no float32 intermediate is used to derive authoritative positions. Final draw projection may use Godot vectors. The leaf creates no native object and calls no mutator, timer, scene or camera.

The original diagram uses these exact EUS-metre points: `[0,0,100]`, `[0,0,-2700]`, `[-1400,0,-2700]`, `[-1400,0,1300]`, `[0,0,1300]`, `[0,0,100]`. Five labels are Departure, Crosswind, Downwind, Base, Final. They are original horizontal illustration dimensions, **not a flight procedure or recommended spacing**. All y0 values mean the map drawing plane, never a target flight altitude. The endpoint is the existing pavement end, not an operational threshold. Do not add altitude/speed gates, compute a safe path, show “turn now,” infer a current leg, automatically advance or grade a circuit.

Scene owns one session-local `circuit_aid_enabled:bool`, initially false. Choice changes only in paused First flight and clears on a fresh session. The map receives a copied view through `set_circuit_reference(view:Dictionary)->void`; disabled means the unavailable shape with reason **Aid off**, never stale points. Draw an available view as an ordinary clipped geographic layer using the existing ownship-centered projection. Preserve the existing bound map toggle/runway/zoom, current extent (including the 1,000m minimum), ownship centering, runway selection and route. Do not auto-zoom, pan, recenter or force the whole 4,000m-tall diagram into this layer; geographic points/labels may be outside the chart. Do not use `set_route` or forge a landmark itinerary. Runway18 or unsupported wind/start makes the reference unavailable without changing the player's selection. Map/card are `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE` during live flight.

`CircuitReferenceCard.set_state(view:Dictionary, enabled:bool)->void` keeps copies only. When enabled and available, it separately fits the complete fixed schematic and all five leg labels inside the reference card at every supported window size, independently of map extent and ownship position. Use the same six fixture points with fixed bounds; label it **Fixed schematic · not to map scale**. The card shows no ownship marker or inferred current leg. Unavailable/off clears the schematic and labels and shows the bounded reason. Its paused chooser emits `enabled_requested(enabled:bool, source_session:String)` under the same current-widget/session and paused-modal guards as Consumer A. The live card has no clickable action. Root integrates this child's checkbox into the briefing without concurrent edits to Consumer A's panel.

## Presentation, recording and source limits

Persistent label: **OPTIONAL SYNTHETIC CIRCUIT REFERENCE · not evaluated**. Reading labels remain native truth/TAS/ellipsoid height/anchor-north as appropriate. Diagram legs and manual software steps never represent actual flight phases, clearance, stable approach, safe altitude or landing success. Do not invent C172 speeds, procedures, manufacturer dimensions, radio calls, sensor behavior or achievements.

ADR011/012 recording and archive remain byte-compatible. No scenario/assist fields are inserted, and no saved review is claimed to contain new orientation/aid context. The briefing states: **Circuit aid choice is session-local and is not stored in saved flight reviews.** Cold review remains unavailable. Pilot commands retain their existing provenance because the aid cannot command flight; visible geometric guidance is separately labeled. No persistent progress, hours, awards or profile state is created.

Use readable focusable standard controls, synchronous tooltips and bounded text at960x540/1920x1080/2560x1440. The paused briefing may cover the cockpit and use readable scrolling or tabs; do not shrink all choices, hints and steps to fit at once. Keep current pause/session status, Back and Controls reachable. Back returns still paused to the ordinary explicit Resume action; the panel gains no Resume signal. During live flight, the optional map/card must preserve primary flight readings, status and look-release access. Explain the existing map visibility binding and how to disable the aid in paused First flight; add no live card action or new binding. Aid off preserves the existing presentation and flight behavior.
